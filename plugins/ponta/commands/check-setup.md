Check my Ponta setup and show me what you can do.

Please run these checks in order with the Ponta tools, and tell me in plain words what you find.
1. Call list_devices with health set. Tell me which of my PCs are online, which tool sets are ready on each, and the Ponta version on each.
2. List what is in the Desktop folder on one of my online PCs. If more than one is online, pick the first and say which. Only list it. Do not open or change anything.
3. If the browser tools are ready, open https://ponta.ai in a new tab in Ponta's browser, tell me the page title, then close that tab. Do not save a snapshot, a console log or a screenshot to disk. Read the title from the tab list, so no file is written.
4. If the mail tools are ready, list my mailboxes. If there are none, say that no mailbox is set up yet.
Do not change, move, send or delete anything during this check.
If the Point tool is ready on that PC, call it at the start of each of steps 2, 3 and 4 for about two seconds. Point at the window or place that step uses, with a few words such as "Listing the Desktop". This lets me see you working on my own PC. If Point is not ready, skip this and carry on.

Then give me a short list of what works and what needs fixing. For each fix, give the matching page from https://ponta.ai/help.
End with these three example jobs I could ask you to do next. Say which ones the tool sets that are ready allow, and that each one asks me before it changes anything:
1. Fill in a PDF form from an email and upload it: Find the newest email in my mailbox with a PDF form attached. Fill in the form with details from that email, and show me the result. Ask me before you save anything. Then open the upload page in my own Chrome and ask me before you upload it.
2. Try an Approve request on your phone: Show me a practice Approve and Deny request on my phone, wait for my answer, and tell me what I chose. Nothing changes on my PC either way.
3. Ask from your phone, run on your PC: From the AI app on my phone: find the five largest files in the Downloads folder on my PC and tell me their names and sizes. Only list them. Do not open, move or change anything.
Last, offer to write a short test note called Ponta check.txt on my Desktop, so I can see a change made on my PC.
Only write it if I say yes. If I do, tell me I can delete it whenever I like.
